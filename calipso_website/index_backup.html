About Calipso

Nestled in the lush surroundings of Playa Avellana in Guanacaste, Costa Rica, Calipso is a charming 6-room eco-friendly jungle lodge offering a peaceful and intimate tropical escape.

Located just a 7-minute walk from beautiful Avellanas Beach, an uncrowded gem with surf breaks for all levels, Calipso is close to everything while remaining your own piece of paradise.

Rooms

Our comfortable rooms provide the perfect place to relax and recharge after a day of surfing, yoga, exploring, or enjoying the beautiful beaches of Guanacaste.

Each room features a private bathroom with shower and hot water, as well as a private patio with a hammock and chairs.

Choose from rooms with or without air conditioning, with queen beds, single beds, and a family room.

Fast and reliable Wi-Fi is available throughout the property.

Property

Enjoy our outdoor swimming pool, pool bar, peaceful garden, and dedicated yoga deck surrounded by tropical nature.

Guests also have access to a fully equipped outdoor shared kitchen with everything needed to prepare your own meals.

Located along the main road and within walking distance of local restaurants and bars, Calipso is quiet, safe, and secure.

Gated, secure parking is available for guests.

Yoga & Surf Retreats

Create a personalized retreat experience by booking the entire property exclusively for your group.

Our intimate 6-room jungle lodge is an ideal setting for private surf retreats, yoga retreats, or a combination of both.

Your retreat can be customized with options including a private chef, customized meals, guided surf sessions, yoga classes, and local excursions.

Contact us for details, availability, and customized retreat pricing.

Explore Guanacaste

Avellanas Beach is approximately a 7-minute walk from the lodge.

Explore the beaches, nature, culture, surf, and attractions of Guanacaste while enjoying Calipso as your relaxed home base.

Liberia International Airport is approximately 1.5 hours away by car.
