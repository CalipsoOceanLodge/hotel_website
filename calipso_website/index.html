About Calipso

Nestled in the lush surroundings of Playa Avellana in Guanacaste, Costa Rica, this charming 6-room eco-friendly jungle lodge offers a peaceful and intimate tropical escape.

Located just a 7-minute walk from beautiful Avellanas Beach, an uncrowded gem with surf breaks for all levels.

Close to everything, yet your own piece of paradise.

Rooms

Our comfortable rooms provide the perfect place to relax and recharge after a day of surfing, yoga, exploring, or enjoying the beautiful beaches of Guanacaste.

Each room features a private bathroom with a shower and hot water, as well as its own private patio with a hammock and chairs.

Guests can choose from rooms with or without air conditioning, with queen beds, single beds, and a family room.

Fast and reliable Wi-Fi is available throughout the property.

Property

Enjoy our outdoor swimming pool, pool bar, tranquil garden area, and dedicated yoga deck surrounded by tropical nature.

The property also offers a fully equipped outdoor shared kitchen with all essential amenities.

Conveniently located along the main road, the lodge is within walking distance of local restaurants and bars.

Our property is quiet, safe and secure, with gated parking.

Yoga & Surf Retreats

Create a personalized retreat experience by booking the entire property exclusively for your group.

Our intimate 6-room jungle lodge is an ideal setting for private surf retreats, yoga retreats, or a combination of both.

Your retreat can be customized to suit your group, with options including a private chef and customized meals, guided surf sessions, yoga classes, and local excursions.

Whether you're looking for an active surf adventure, a restorative yoga escape, or the perfect combination of wellness, adventure, and tropical relaxation, we can help create a retreat tailored to your group.

Contact us for more details, availability, and customized retreat pricing.

Explore Guanacaste

Avellanas Beach is approximately a 7-minute walk from the lodge, making it easy to spend your days surfing, swimming, relaxing, or exploring the coast.

Explore the beaches, nature, culture, and attractions of Guanacaste while enjoying Calipso as your relaxed home base.

Liberia International Airport is approximately 1.5 hours away by car.
