WELCOME TO CALIPSO

About Calipso Ocean Lodge

Nestled in the lush surroundings of Playa Avellana in Guanacaste, Costa Rica, this charming 6-room eco-friendly jungle lodge offers a peaceful and intimate tropical escape. Located just a 7-minute walk (approximately 550–700 meters) from beautiful Avellanas Beach, an uncrowded gem with surf breaks for all levels.

The lodge features an outdoor swimming pool, pool bar, tranquil garden area, and a dedicated yoga deck—perfect for morning practice, relaxation, and enjoying the surrounding nature.

The property also offers a fully equipped outdoor shared kitchen with all essential amenities, giving guests the flexibility to prepare their own meals. Conveniently located along the main road, the lodge is within walking distance of local restaurants and bars. Our property is quiet, safe and secure. Close to everything, yet your own piece of paradise. Liberia International Airport is about 1.5 hours by car, making the lodge an ideal base for enjoying all the best of what Guanacaste has to offer.

STAY A WHILE

Our Rooms

Our comfortable rooms provide the perfect place to relax and recharge after a day of surfing, yoga, exploring, or enjoying the beautiful beaches of Guanacaste. Each room features a private bathroom with a shower and hot water, as well as its own private patio with a hammock and chairs—ideal for unwinding in the peaceful surroundings.

Guests can choose from rooms with or without air conditioning, with a variety of configurations including queen beds, single beds, and a family room to accommodate different needs. Fast and reliable Wi-Fi is also available throughout the property, allowing you to stay connected while enjoying your tropical getaway.
